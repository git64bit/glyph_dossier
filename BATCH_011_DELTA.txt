PURPOSE:
  Add BOSL2 region-based occupied-cell detection to the accepted
  generic multi-family sectioning route.

AUTHORITY:
  - exact captured BOSL2 glyph region
  - exact-height normalized region
  - resolved automatic or manual rectangular grid
  - BOSL2 function intersection()
  - BOSL2 region_area() and region_parts()

OCCUPANCY CONTRACT:
  - each cell receives one immutable occupancy record
  - clipped region = glyph region intersected with cell rectangle
  - occupied area is measured from the clipped BOSL2 region
  - occupied when area > configured area epsilon
  - zero-area or boundary-only contact is treated as empty
  - occupied and empty records remain row-major and deterministic
  - occupied-only layout and export use recorded clipped regions
  - no geometry sampling or raster approximation
