# Character taxonomy

The registry is organized by recurring geometry rather than alphabetic
order alone.

## Principal structural families

- **Rings and bowls:** O, o, 0, D, P, B, 8.
- **Open curves:** C, c, G.
- **Reversing curves:** S, s, 3.
- **Long diagonals:** N, Z, 2, 7.
- **Acute vertices:** A, V, W, v, w.
- **Crossings and forks:** X, Y, K, x, y, k.
- **Repeated arches:** m and n.
- **Ascender and descender pairs:** b/d and p/q.
- **Disconnected components:** i, j, ?, !, :, and ;.
- **Font-form variants:** a, g, 1, 4, 7, 0.

## Why individual dossiers precede sectioning

A cut that is harmless in H can destroy:

- the apex of A;
- the waist of S or 8;
- the dot relationship in i or ?;
- the lower loop of a two-storey g;
- the optional counter in a closed 4;
- the distinction between 0 and O.

The sectioning design must therefore emerge from observed character
anatomy rather than from a universal rectangular grid alone.
