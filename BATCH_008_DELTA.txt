PURPOSE:
  Add set-ID plus glyph-ID selection to the portable catalog and store
  several materially different portable font families.

PORTABLE FAMILIES:
  - Liberation Sans Regular R1 (existing)
  - Montserrat Regular R1
  - Alpha Slab One Regular R1
  - Fira Sans Regular R1
  - Miama Nueva Medium R1
  - Playfair Display Regular R1

PACKAGE CONTRACT:
  - 66 dossier glyphs per family.
  - Namespaced generated constants prevent cross-family collisions.
  - Exact family lookup precedes exact glyph lookup.
  - Source filename, license, provenance, SHA-256, and version recorded.
  - Every new package has a 66-glyph immutable package lock.
  - Source font binaries are not packaged; extracted contours are portable.

WORKBENCHES:
  - portable_catalog.scad: family plus glyph selection.
  - category sheets: family selection.
  - portable_family_comparison.scad: same glyph across all six families.

UNCHANGED:
  - Liberation Sans package contents.
  - Portable U_A normalization and A-only sectioning.
  - Connectors, attachment methods, and printing.
